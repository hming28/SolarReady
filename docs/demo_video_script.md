## Recording Checklist for the Team

1. **Resolution**: Record at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps or 30fps.
2. **Browser Window**: Use Chrome/Edge in Full Screen mode (F11) with bookmarks and search bar hidden.
3. **Cursor**: Enable smooth cursor pointer with subtle click effects.
4. **Clean State**: Ensure no console errors or extraneous dev tools appear in the frame.
5. **Pacing**: Follow the script line-by-line; leave 1 second of pause between section transitions for easy video editing.
